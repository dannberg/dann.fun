01
Tuesday, December 23
Arrival
NRT → Tokyo
Afternoon

Land at Narita. Take the Narita Express (N'EX) to Shinjuku or Tokyo Station (~90 min). Check into hotel, get oriented, pick up your Suica IC card at the airport.

Evening

Food Stroll Shinjuku — Golden Gai for tiny atmospheric bars, or Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane) for yakitori under the train tracks. Welcome to Japan.

Chill mode → Jet-lagged? Japanese convenience store dinner (7-Eleven onigiri and egg sandwiches are legitimately great) and early sleep.
02
Wednesday, December 24
Tokyo East Side
Asakusa · Akihabara
Morning

History Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa — one of Tokyo's oldest temples. Walk Nakamise-dori shopping street for snacks and souvenirs.

Late Morning

Animals Cafe Capyba (Asakusa location) — reservation-only, 30 min with two capybaras. Books 2 weeks ahead at cspace.co.jp. Set a reminder for Dec 9 to book. 5-min walk from Asakusa Station (TX line).

Afternoon

Culture Akihabara — retro gaming paradise. Hit Super Potato and BEEP for vintage games, browse the arcades and figure shops.

Evening

Food Christmas Eve is a couples' holiday in Japan — perfect for a nice dinner. Consider booking a sushi omakase in advance for tonight.

Chill mode → Skip Akihabara. Slow morning at Senso-ji, capybaras, then a long leisurely Christmas Eve dinner.
03
Thursday, December 25
The Denim Pilgrimage
Hachioji · Harajuku · Shibuya
Morning

Denim Iron Heart HQ — JR Chuo Line from Shinjuku to Hachioji (~45 min), then taxi (~¥2,000). Open Wed–Sun, 11:00–18:00. Budget a couple hours to try on heavyweight denim and talk shop. The staff can call you a taxi back to the station when you're done.

Afternoon

Culture Back in central Tokyo — Harajuku & Omotesando. Takeshita Street for the spectacle, backstreets of Ura-Harajuku for quieter boutiques. Backup capybara plan: Capyneko Harajuku on Takeshita St (capybara + cats).

Evening

Food Shibuya Crossing at night, then dinner in Ebisu — Ebisu Yokocho food hall has great atmosphere and affordable izakaya vibes.

Chill mode → Iron Heart is a trek — if it feels like too much today, swap it to Day 6 (your flex day). They're also open Sunday.
04
Friday, December 26
Snow Monkeys
Tokyo → Nagano → Tokyo
Early Morning

Hokuriku Shinkansen from Tokyo Station to Nagano (~80 min). Then Nagaden Express Bus to the park (~40 min). Budget an early start — aim to catch the 8 or 9am bus.

Midday

Animals Jigokudani Monkey Park — 30-min forest walk to the hot spring pool. Late December should have snow on the ground for the iconic bathing scenes. The monkeys are wild, unafraid of humans, and endlessly entertaining. Budget 1.5–2 hours. Wear waterproof boots with grip.

Afternoon

History Back in Nagano City, visit Zenko-ji Temple — one of Japan's most important Buddhist temples, founded in the 7th century. Walk the atmospheric approach road lined with shops.

Evening

Shinkansen back to Tokyo. Grab dinner near your hotel — you'll have earned a big bowl of ramen tonight.

Chill mode → Stay overnight in Yudanaka or Shibu Onsen near the monkey park (tattoo-friendly ryokan available) and return the next morning. Would require adjusting Day 5.
05
Saturday, December 27
Hakone & the Ryokan
Tokyo → Hakone (overnight)
Morning

Pack light for one night. Take the Odakyu Romance Car from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto (~85 min) — scenic reserved-seat express through the mountains.

Afternoon

Culture Hakone sightseeing loop — Lake Ashi cruise with Mt. Fuji views (clear winter days are best), Owakudani volcanic valley (eat a black egg for 7 extra years of life), Hakone Open-Air Museum for sculpture in the mountains.

Evening

Onsen Check into your tattoo-friendly ryokan. Top picks with private onsen:

Gora Kansuiro — mid-high range in Gora. Tattoo-friendly in public and private baths. Incredible kaiseki food.
Kinnotake Tonosawa — higher-end. Only has private outdoor onsen — no tattoo concerns whatsoever.
Hakone Ginyu — upscale. Every room has its own private onsen on the terrace.

Kaiseki dinner at the ryokan, then soak in your private onsen under the stars. This is the night.

06
Sunday, December 28
Flex Day
Hakone → Tokyo
Morning

Onsen One more soak. Leisurely ryokan checkout and breakfast. If you missed anything in Hakone, catch it now.

Afternoon

Romance Car back to Shinjuku. Use the afternoon for anything you missed or want to revisit — Tsukiji Outer Market for street food, Ginza for upscale shopping, Meiji Shrine for a peaceful forest walk in the middle of the city.

Evening

Food This is your big omakase night. Book something special in the Ginza area. This is the splurge meal.

Chill mode → Make this a wandering day. Get lost in Shimokitazawa (vintage shops, cafes, live music) or Yanaka (old Tokyo charm, cat street). Iron Heart backup: they're open Sunday too.
07
Monday, December 29
Ancient Capital
Tokyo → Kyoto
Morning

Hikari Shinkansen from Tokyo to Kyoto (~2h 20min). Store luggage at hotel or use a coin locker at the station.

Afternoon

History Higashiyama district — visit Kiyomizu-dera Temple, a stunning hilltop temple with panoramic city views. Walk down through the atmospheric stone-paved lanes of Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka, lined with tea houses and craft shops.

Evening

Food Gion district — Kyoto's geisha quarter. Stroll Hanami-koji for a chance to spot geiko and maiko. Dinner on Pontocho Alley, a narrow lane along the Kamogawa river packed with restaurants.

Chill mode → Just do Gion wandering and dinner. Save temples for tomorrow.
08
Tuesday, December 30
Gates, Deer & Gold
Kyoto · Nara · Kyoto
Early Morning

History Fushimi Inari Shrine — thousands of vermillion torii gates winding up a mountainside. Go early (7–8am) for emptier trails and better photos. The full hike is ~2 hours, but even the first section is spectacular.

Late Morning

Animals Train to Nara (~45 min). Visit Todai-ji Temple which houses a massive bronze Buddha, then wander Nara Park where hundreds of sacred deer roam free. Buy shika senbei (deer crackers) — the deer will bow to you for them.

Afternoon

History Back to Kyoto for Kinkaku-ji (the Golden Pavilion) — especially beautiful reflected in the pond on a clear winter day.

Evening

Food Final night dinner — try Kyoto specialties: yudofu (hot pot tofu, a winter classic), or splurge on a full Kyoto kaiseki experience.

Chill mode → Skip either Nara or Kinkaku-ji, not both. Nara is really special — the deer alone are worth the trip.
09
Wednesday, December 31
Departure & New Year's Eve
Kyoto → NRT
If Afternoon / Evening Flight

Culture Morning visit to Arashiyama — the famous bamboo grove, Togetsukyo Bridge, and Iwatayama Monkey Park (wild monkeys on a mountain, different vibe from Jigokudani). Then shinkansen to Tokyo and N'EX to Narita.

If Early Flight

Straight to NRT. Shinkansen Kyoto → Tokyo (~2h 20min) + N'EX to Narita (~1h). Budget 4.5–5 hours total door-to-gate.

New Year's Eve Note

History If your flight is late enough — Japanese New Year (Oshogatsu) preparations will be everywhere. Temples ring bells 108 times at midnight. It's magical.

Logistics & Tips

🚄 Getting Around

Skip the JR Pass. At ¥50,000 for 7 days, it doesn't pencil out for this itinerary. Buy individual shinkansen tickets via the SmartEX app or at station machines. You also get to ride the faster Nozomi trains this way.

Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card at Narita — works on all local trains, buses, and convenience stores.

Shinjuku → Hakone (Romance Car)~¥2,500

♨️ Tattoo Tips

Tattoos are a non-issue in everyday life — restaurants, shops, trains, all fine. The concern is only with shared bathing facilities (onsen, sento).

  • We've planned private onsen at the Hakone ryokan — zero stress
  • For public baths in Tokyo, search for "tattoo-friendly sento" — they exist but require research
  • Some places offer waterproof tattoo cover patches if needed
  • Tenzan Onsen in Hakone is tattoo-friendly for day-use bathing

🌡️ Weather & Packing

Late December Tokyo: cold but mild (~5–10°C / 41–50°F). Nagano will be significantly colder with snow. Hakone and Kyoto similar to Tokyo.

  • Layers + a good warm coat
  • Waterproof boots with grip — essential for the snow monkey park forest trail
  • Portable battery pack (cold drains phone batteries fast)
  • Cash — Japan is still quite cash-heavy outside major chains
